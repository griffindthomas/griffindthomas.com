Boston to Copenhagen to Malpensa on SAS.

## Milan

The 27th to the 29th. The Duomo, the Pinacoteca Ambrosiana, Sant'Ambrogio, and San Bernardino alle Ossa, which is a small chapel off a side street with its walls lined floor to ceiling in human bone. Nothing about the outside of it tells you that.

## North to the lake

Picked the rental car up at Malpensa on the 29th and stopped at the Pagani factory on the way north.

## Switzerland

The 30th, over the border and back in a day: St. Moritz, Lake Staz, Diavolezza, the Bernina Pass, and down the other side to Poschiavo.

## Villa del Balbianello

A private tour on the 1st that put us in thirty minutes ahead of general admission, so the terraces were empty. Then a private boat out to the houseboat the museum also owns, and back again.

Arriving by water is the whole thing. The Naboo scenes in Attack of the Clones were shot on those terraces, and coming in off the lake with them stacked above you is that shot from the inside. It felt like Anakin and Padme and I am not going to pretend it did not.
