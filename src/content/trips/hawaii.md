Landed at Kona and took the bus around to Whittington Beach Park. That is most of a day on public transport with five days of camping gear on your back, and it sets the tone for the rest of it.

## Kilauea

Nine in the morning until nine at night, across Hawaii Volcanoes National Park. Kilauea and Kilauea Iki, twenty miles, with a forty pound pack the whole way.

The floor of Iki is a lava lake that crusted over in 1959 and it has not finished cooling. Steam still comes up out of the cracks in it, and the rock is warm through your boots in places.

This is the one hike in the log with no watch behind it. The distance is right and the elevation is a guess, which is why it carries a tilde on the hiking page.

## The bus I missed

The last day was supposed to finish with snorkelling at Kealakekua Bay. I missed a bus in Hilo and there was no second one that got me there in time, so that was the end of that plan. Hilo straight through to Kona instead, an Uber to the airport, and out.
