Built as a senior honours project. The tunnel is open-circuit, which is the
version you can actually fit on a desk, and the test section is sized around
printed airfoil sections rather than the other way around.

The interesting part is not the fan. It is the contraction and the settling
section ahead of the test area, which is what decides whether the flow arriving
at the airfoil is worth looking at. Get that wrong and the visualisation shows
you the tunnel rather than the wing.
